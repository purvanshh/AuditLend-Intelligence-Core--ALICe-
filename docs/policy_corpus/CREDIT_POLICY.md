# AuditLend Credit Policy Manual
Version: 2026.1
Effective: 2026-01-01

## 1.0 General Lending Principles
1.1 All lending decisions must be auditable and explainable.
1.2 Risk score, data reliability, and confidence are separate concepts.
1.3 The audit trail is the source of truth for all decisions.

## 2.0 Borrower Eligibility
2.1 Minimum monthly income: ₹25,000
2.2 Minimum credit score: 600 (or equivalent bureau score)
2.3 Maximum DTI: 50% for unsecured loans
2.4 GST registration required for loans above ₹5,00,000

## 3.0 Credit Score Requirements
3.1 Score >= 780: Grade A (preferred terms)
3.2 Score >= 720: Grade B (standard terms)
3.3 Score >= 660: Grade C (standard terms)
3.4 Score >= 600: Grade D (higher rate)
3.5 Score < 600: Decline

## 4.0 Debt-to-Income Policy
4.1 Maximum DTI for automatic approval: 35%
4.2 DTI between 35% and 50%: Manual review required
4.3 DTI > 50%: Decline
4.4 Self-employed borrowers: DTI calculated on 6-month average income
4.5 DTI = (total monthly obligations) / (monthly income)

## 5.0 Income Verification
5.1 Salaried: Last 3 months salary slips required
5.2 Self-employed: Last 6 months bank statements + IT returns
5.3 Income stability score < 0.3: Additional verification required

## 6.0 GST Compliance
6.1 GST-compliant borrowers get priority processing
6.2 PAN-GST mismatch: Automatic manual review
6.3 GST non-compliance caps risk score at 54 (below auto-approve threshold)

## 7.0 Loan Amount Guidelines
7.1 Maximum loan amount: 24x monthly income (unsecured)
7.2 Minimum loan amount: ₹10,000
7.3 Loan-to-income ratio > 40%: Manual review

## 8.0 Manual Review Criteria
8.1 Confidence < 0.6: Route to manual review
8.2 GST non-compliance: Route to manual review
8.3 DTI > 35% with credit score < 660: Route to manual review
8.4 All manual reviews must be completed within 48 hours

## 9.0 Fraud and Risk Indicators
9.1 Multiple applications within 30 days: Flag for review
9.2 Income mismatch between declared and bank-derived: Flag for review
9.3 Recent credit inquiries > 6 in 6 months: Additional scrutiny

## 10.0 Regulatory Compliance
10.1 All decisions must comply with RBI Fair Practices Code
10.2 Interest rate must not exceed usury limits
10.3 Borrower must receive clear, itemized explanation of decision
